# FCCD High School v1.1.5 + Prestige-Based Dynasty v1.0.4 — Combined Mods

**The updated combined mods are available for Windows!** This release corrects a preseason recruiting issue in the Prestige code while retaining all existing High School v1.1.5 features.

### What's fixed
- AI teams can no longer issue scholarship offers during Spring and Summer preseason, matching the restriction on human-controlled teams.
- AI scholarship offers and recruiting commitments resume normally during the regular season.
- All other Prestige recruiting rules, roster limits and High School gameplay rules remain intact.

### What's included
- **Ohio High School Divisions I–VII**, each with its own combined installer
- **Generic High School**, offering Large, Medium and Small school profiles
- 12-minute quarters, kickoffs from the 40-yard line and high-school touchback rules
- Returnable missed field goals, Kick Power-based distance and game-clock adjustments
- No redshirts, four-year eligibility and state-championship terminology
- Prestige-based 34–85 player roster limits, scaled scholarship offers, AI roster management and walk-on generation

### Testing
**Ohio Division III, Ohio Division V and one Generic High School profile passed gameplay tests.** All three showed zero scholarship offers through Summer 2, followed by normal AI scholarship offers and recruiting commitments by Week 5. The other five Ohio division packages passed technical file, game-core checksum and JavaScript syntax checks but were not individually gameplay-tested. The three Generic school-size profiles have not all been separately tested in-game.

### Installation
1. Back up important dynasty saves and close FCCD.
2. Restore the game to **verified stock Windows Steam FCCD v1.6.5** before installing a combined mod.
3. Download **one** of the eight ZIPs below and extract all contents.
4. Run its top-level `INSTALL.bat`, then `VERIFY.bat`.
5. To uninstall or change packages, close the game and run the matching `RESTORE.bat`.

**Each ZIP contains both High School and Prestige features. Do not install standalone mods on top of a combined package.** The preseason fix is already included; no separate hotfix is needed.

macOS compatibility has not been verified. Check redistribution permissions for any bundled third-party game assets before publishing.
